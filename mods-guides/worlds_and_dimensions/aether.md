# 🐾 Cobblemon Spawns in the Aether

{% hint style="success" %}
The current v6.0 datapack defines hundreds of Aether-specific spawn rules, with around 190 to 200 distinct Pokémon species or forms available in each Skyroot biome.
{% endhint %}

The dimension is especially known for:

- 🌈 Fairy- and Flying-type Pokémon
- 🐉 Dragons and pseudo-legendaries
- 🌿 Starter Pokémon and rare evolutions
- ⚠️ Paradox Pokémon
- 👽 Ultra Beasts
- 🏆 Legendary and Mythical Pokémon

{% hint style="warning" %}
Many Ghost-type and dragon encounters require low sky light or complete darkness. Some Pokémon also depend on daytime, rain, water, flowers, Saccharine Trees, or other nearby blocks.
{% endhint %}

# 🌈 The Identity of the Aether in Cobblemon Realms

The four Skyroot biomes share a large core encounter pool, but each biome also has its own Ultra Rare selection. Meadow and Grove favor open-area starters, fossils, Paradox Pokémon, and legendary encounters, while Forest and Woodland focus more heavily on woodland species, Ultra Beasts, and late-game Pokémon.

# 🚪 How to Access the Aether

## ✅ Portal Activation

1. Build a frame using **Glowstone**
2. Pour water into the center
3. Walk through the portal

![Aether Portal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LevelsCraft7/cobblemon-realms-wiki/refs/heads/main/imgwiki/The-Aether/Portail_Aether.jpg)

{% hint style="success" %}
Unlike the Nether or The Otherside, the Aether can be accessed very early in a playthrough.
{% endhint %}
